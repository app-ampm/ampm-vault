# AMPM Platform — Assumptions We Took on Your Behalf

**For:** AMPM review · **Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Status:** AMPM's decisions of 2026-10-25 applied; the last two columns show what AMPM decided for each line.

Your brief says the developer must not invent business policies. We respected that by drafting the design with explicit assumptions wherever a question was open, each tagged with the question it stands in for. This sheet lists all of them in plain groups. Lines marked Changed or Dropped are being reworked in the design; lines marked Still open or Partly decided wait for the items AMPM listed to confirm; the rest stand as written. The Depends on column names the audit question (Q), finding (GP, CF, AM) or earlier assumption each line rests on; the questions themselves are in [the open decisions document](open-decisions.md).

Identifier prefixes: **A** data model, **P** permissions, **T** technology, **U** user flows, **W** screens, **V** testing.
