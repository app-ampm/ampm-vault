Questions
What happens when my month ends?Your saved hotels and your enquiries stay readable. Nothing is deleted, and renewing brings everything back.
Who creates my account?AMPM does, after we've spoken. You'll get an invitation, sign in with your mobile number, add a few wedding details and pay by card or UPI.
Are the prices final?They're indicative ranges, each with the date we last checked it. For your dates, ask for availability and the hotel team confirms with the property.

ABOUT AMPMOne team, from the first call to the last departure.
AMPM plans destination weddings in India. We started with hotels: knowing which ones work for a wedding, what they really cost, and who to call.That knowledge is now a database you can search yourself. When you want more, the same team runs the rest, function by function.
Dated, not guessedEvery hotel rate carries the date AMPM last checked it. When a rate is old, we say so.
Nothing without your yesDesigns, menus, proposals and invitations come to you as versions. You approve the exact version, and it doesn't change after.
Ready before it's liveNo function starts until its checklist is complete, or an authorised manager has signed off an exception.
