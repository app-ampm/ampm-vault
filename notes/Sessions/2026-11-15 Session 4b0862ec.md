## Narrative

A side session started from the enquiry-workflow session. Staff could see that an enquiry was "Unassigned" but could not assign anyone, so every enquiry stayed unassigned. Decision: [[D-133 Hotel enquiries get an owner from the staff who can work every enquiry]].

**Decided.** The people who can hold an enquiry are the active staff whose roles grant `consultancy.enquiries.update` on every record. The policy engine decides this through a new `PrincipalService.staffHolding`, custom roles included, rather than a hard-coded list of role codes. The picker gets a narrow `GET /consultancy-admin/enquiries/assignees` that returns `{ id, name }[]` and is keyed on the update grant, instead of the full staff directory. The PATCH now refuses an owner who fails the same check; before, it stored any uuid. Project managers are left out because their grant reaches only their own weddings. The Mine / Unassigned filters cover open enquiries only, so each list matches its count.

**Built.**
- API: the assignees endpoint, the owner check, `assignedUserName` on the staff views, `mineOpen` on the pipeline, and `assignee=me|unassigned` on the list.
- api-client: `useUpdateStaffEnquiry` and `useEnquiryAssignees`.
- Mobile: an Owner card on the enquiry Overview (picker, Assign to me, back to nobody); the holder's short name on the queue card, row and heading; Everyone / Mine / Unassigned chips kept in the URL beside the stage (built on #141, now `queue-filter.ts`).
- Tests at every layer, including a new e2e. Checked in the web app at 1440 and 375.

**Remains.**
- The PR needs CI, merging and a full API-first redeploy (response contract change; no migration).
- An "assigned" event on the enquiry's Activity tab would need a new event type (a migration).
- Project managers as holders would need a per-enquiry list keyed on the enquiry's project.
