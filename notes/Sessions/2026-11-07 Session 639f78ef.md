## Narrative

**Asked:** implement the approved Peacock Night design system (designer handoff in ~/Downloads, now kept at `reference_docs/design/peacock-night/`), customise the component library to it, rebuild the existing frontend, and leave it ready for the screens still to come.

**Decided (with why):**
- Peacock Night adopted tokens-first ([[D-076 Peacock Night adopted as the design system — four colours, glass recipes, tokens-first]]): four colours only, as the same semantic CSS variables plus an opacity-step vocabulary (Tailwind v3 drops any other `/n` silently), glass recipes instead of drop shadows, Gilda Display / Manrope / Rozha One, dark only. The contrast gate became data (`contrastPairs`).
- Midnight on peacock measures 4.25:1, not the 4.5:1 the handoff claimed: kept as approved with a documented exception, pending AMPM's call on `#148F8A` (4.53:1) ([[D-077 Midnight on peacock at 4.25 to 1 kept as approved, pending AMPM's call on 148F8A]]).
- App shell ([[D-078 App shell — floating tab bar on phones, staff rail and client top bar from 768, areas from policy grants]]): screens own the floating tab bar, the layout draws the staff rail or the client top bar from 768, staff areas come from `@ampm/policy` grants.
- The Next.js site gets its own preset (next/font variables, the `web:` variant) and reuses the library's class recipes through `@ampm/ui/styles`.
