**Feature 8.2 — convert lead → client account + portal invite.** On the developer's go, built the convert action end to end. Studied the Module B design package for 8.2 through a seven-reader parallel workflow (data model, architecture boundary, AC-01.8/AC-12.3/AC-21, Flow 1 + wireframes, the `crm.accounts` policy keys, the identity/invite seam, and the 8.1 conventions): `client_accounts`/`account_contacts` were already in the ERD + field-matrix, the `Lead` already carried `converted_account_id`, and the `crm.accounts.*` keys were already generated (no authz drift). A conflict between two reader briefs over whether the keys existed was resolved deterministically — they live in `capabilities.json` (rows in `matrix.json`).

**Decided.** Convert is a service-level system invariant (not a policy status machine), guarded by the existing `crm.accounts.create` key — no new verb. Module B must not write the kernel `users` table (api.md), so added a kernel `ClientUserService.provisionInvitedClient`, called alongside `AuthTokenService.issueInvite` and `NotificationService.queue`; the invitee activates through the existing `/auth/activate` flow (no new acceptance code). Billing (RESTRICTED-BANK) is never selected for the staff view and the client view is a separate `.strict()` schema; `internal_notes` (RESTRICTED-NOTES) is staff-only. The lead↔account link is the ERD's bidirectional pair, the account owning the `@unique` `lead_id` (one account per lead; a concurrent double-convert trips it → 409).

**Feature 8.3 — subscription purchase → Razorpay → activation → entitlement gating (AC-02), the commercial payoff of the CRM slice.** On the developer's go — after verifying the 31-Oct hardening pass (PR #32) had landed and fast-forwarding this worktree onto it — built the subscription backend end to end. Studied the Module B design package through a seven-reader parallel workflow (data model, entitlements/policy keys, payment adapter, jobs/webhook, AC-02, crm conventions, flow + config pricing): the four commercial tables and the `crm.plans`/`crm.subscriptions`/`crm.payments_inbound` keys were already curated, the entitlement resolver already encodes §3, and a cross-reader enum conflict (`plan_modules.module_key`) was resolved against the policy vocabulary.

**Decided.** No new runtime dependency — not even the Razorpay SDK: HMAC-SHA256 via `node:crypto`, orders via `fetch`, behind one `PaymentAdapter` with a stub chosen by `PAYMENT_PROVIDER` config (the bot-check pattern; prod refuses the stub). The signed webhook is verified at the edge over the raw body (401 and nothing written on a bad signature) and processed by an idempotent worker job; the Finance manual bank transfer runs the SAME activation step. Idempotency is DB-anchored (`payments.provider_ref` unique + a `WHERE status = pending_payment` guard + a `webhook_events` ingest store), never Redis-only. Entitlements stay computed: `principal.service.ts` now derives a client's subscription state from the account's live subscription and busts the ≤5-min cache on activation and expiry. Money crosses the wire as integer paise; the price lives in a `plans` row; a new `scan_price_literals.py` CI check fails the build on a hard-coded price (AC-02.9/25.4). D-recorded the pattern (D-059) and added a `webhook_events` note to data-model.md §4.

**Built and reviewed.** The migration (five models), the `@ampm/core` contracts, the `crm/subscriptions` module (public catalogue, staff purchase → order, webhook, manual activation, expiry sweep, entitlement wiring), the seeded Discovery plan, and the AC-02 e2e set + a payment-adapter contract test. `pnpm check` green (27/27). A five-lens adversarial review (+ a verify pass) confirmed six findings (deduped to four): fixed the three mediums — a webhook enqueue-failure strand (now self-heals by re-driving a `received` event), a non-atomic/non-idempotent purchase (now reuses a pending subscription), and unaudited payment rows (now §9-audited on create and on the succeeded/failed transition) — each with a new regression test. The fourth (per-process entitlement-cache invalidation not crossing API↔worker in production) is within the documented 5-min TTL and is noted as a follow-up (needs cross-process invalidation).

**State / landed.** Feature 8.3 was pushed, opened as **PR #33**, and **merged to main** (merge commit `94c3443`, 1 Nov 2026) with the `Monday: crm progress` trailer — the card sync ran green.
