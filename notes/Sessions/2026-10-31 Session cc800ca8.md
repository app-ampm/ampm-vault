**Feature 8.2 — convert lead → client account + portal invite.** On the developer's go, built the convert action end to end. Studied the Module B design package for 8.2 through a seven-reader parallel workflow (data model, architecture boundary, AC-01.8/AC-12.3/AC-21, Flow 1 + wireframes, the `crm.accounts` policy keys, the identity/invite seam, and the 8.1 conventions): `client_accounts`/`account_contacts` were already in the ERD + field-matrix, the `Lead` already carried `converted_account_id`, and the `crm.accounts.*` keys were already generated (no authz drift). A conflict between two reader briefs over whether the keys existed was resolved deterministically — they live in `capabilities.json` (rows in `matrix.json`).

**Decided.** Convert is a service-level system invariant (not a policy status machine), guarded by the existing `crm.accounts.create` key — no new verb. Module B must not write the kernel `users` table (api.md), so added a kernel `ClientUserService.provisionInvitedClient`, called alongside `AuthTokenService.issueInvite` and `NotificationService.queue`; the invitee activates through the existing `/auth/activate` flow (no new acceptance code). Billing (RESTRICTED-BANK) is never selected for the staff view and the client view is a separate `.strict()` schema; `internal_notes` (RESTRICTED-NOTES) is staff-only. The lead↔account link is the ERD's bidirectional pair, the account owning the `@unique` `lead_id` (one account per lead; a concurrent double-convert trips it → 409).

**Built.** The migration (`client_accounts`/`account_contacts`/`ClientAccountStatus`), the `@ampm/core` accounts contracts, the `crm/accounts` module (convert + GET detail/list), the lead `markConverted` method, and tests (3 core + 6 e2e). `pnpm check` green (27/27; the 9868-test authz suite covers `crm.accounts`).
