## Narrative

Started from the "Mobile apps status and locked decisions" session to retire Amazon Cognito (D-142's follow-up; production-readiness §4 "Retire Cognito"), recorded as **D-153**, PR #167.

**Decided and why.**
- **Built on #165, then rebased.** The work started from `feat/native-builds` (`2842f68`), because both change `apps/mobile/package.json` and `pnpm-lock.yaml` and the native scripts used to verify the change only existed there. #165 merged during the session (`ce239eb`, identical tree), so #167 was rebased onto main (`fdf43fd`) and retargeted.
- **Removed the whole Cognito-shaped seam, not just the adapters.** The provisioner every invite called (a no-op without Cognito) is gone. The nullable sign-in keys and the 404-under-Cognito guards in five auth services are gone. So are the staff sign-in steps only Cognito produced (forced new password, enrolling an authenticator at sign-in), because enrolment happens at activation.
- **`IDENTITY_PROVIDER` defaults to `ampm`.** Production already sets it explicitly, and dev, e2e, CI and local Compose set `local`. A leftover `cognito` fails at boot.
- **`react-native-get-random-values` and AsyncStorage also go.** Nothing imports them, and the exported bundles show no `getRandomValues` caller. `react-native-qrcode-svg` and `expo-secure-store` stay.
- **decisions.json keeps the 26 October text as history.** Q13 and Q14 get a dated 20 November sentence, the A-13, T-2 and T-6 notes say Cognito was replaced, and the generators rebuilt the logs.

**Built and verified.** `pnpm check` (API unit and e2e on a private `ampm_retire_cognito_test` database, mobile 1166 tests), `format:check` and `bundle:check` are green. The bundles carry no Amplify and shrink by about a tenth. `native:prebuild` drops exactly three pods (`AmplifyRTNCore`, `RNCAsyncStorage`, `react-native-get-random-values`), and Android autolinks none of them. A development build ran on the iPhone 17 simulator, using Metro on 8083 because 8082 serves the native-builds worktree.

**What remains.**
- The developer deletes the AWS user pool `ap-south-1_wcaro5oAK`, its app client and the `ampm-cognito-pretoken` Lambda (with its role and log group) in the console, and can drop the box's ignored `COGNITO_*` lines.
- Merge #167 once CI is green. The draft Jest 30 PR #166 touches the same lockfile, so whichever lands second regenerates it.
