Answer given from the design package after AMPM's 25 October decisions (Q1, A-1, T-1, W-3 in decisions.json, architecture.md section 0a, wireframes.md section 0a): two native apps built from one React Native (Expo) codebase, each shipped on iOS and Android, so four store builds.
