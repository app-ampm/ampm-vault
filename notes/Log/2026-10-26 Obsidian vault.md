# Obsidian vault

The project folder was set up as an Obsidian vault. Generated notes live under notes/ and are rebuilt by docs/tools/vault_build.py; open items and log notes are written once and kept. The source of truth for decisions stays docs/decisions.json: record an answer in the open item's note, then update the JSON and rebuild.
