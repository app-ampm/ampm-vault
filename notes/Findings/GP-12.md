# GP-12 One hotel per wedding

**Kind.** Gap (requirements audit, 23 October 2026).

`wedding_projects.hotel_id` is single-valued. Destination weddings commonly use a main hotel plus overflow hotels, and functions may be at venues outside AMPM's database (a farmhouse, a palace, the client's own venue). `functions.venue` is untyped.

## How the design resolved it

`project_hotels`, `external_venues`, `functions.venue_kind` (data model §20.2)
