# GP-26 Client-side users

**Kind.** Gap (requirements audit, 23 October 2026).

SPEC §2.3 "authorised family/decision-maker contacts" and §13 "who approves" imply several client users per wedding with different rights (viewer vs approver; who sees money). Only one client role exists (§6) and `project_members` is not stated to cover client users.

## How the design resolved it

`project_members` client roles, `can_view_finance`, `can_approve` (data model §20.2)
