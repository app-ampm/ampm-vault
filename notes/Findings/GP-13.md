# GP-13 Guest atomic unit

**Kind.** Gap (requirements audit, 23 October 2026).

`guests` has `family_group_id` but there is no family_groups table; `guest_function_attendance` is per guest yet carries `party_count`; `room_allocations` and `travel_segments` use a polymorphic "guest/group_id". Rooming and identity handling need individuals; headcounts need groups including children. The model must choose: the individual guest is the atomic record and the family group is a grouping.

## How the design resolved it

`guests` per person, `family_groups`, occupants and passengers per guest (data model §20.2)
