# GP-19 Statuses

**Kind.** Gap (requirements audit, 23 October 2026).

No status list exists for vendor assignment, vendor proposal, design version, menu version, invitation, RSVP response, payment, invoice, contract, service agreement, travel segment or transfer, or guest ticket. For the ten objects that do have lists (§7), there are no allowed transitions and no rule on who may transition.

## How the design resolved it

every status enumeration is listed on its table; transitions are in permission matrix §7 (data model §20.2)
