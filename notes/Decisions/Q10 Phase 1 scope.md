# Q10 Phase 1 scope

**Tier.** Tier 1, blocking design sign-off.
**Status.** Decided.

## The question

Confirm whether Phase 1 includes the demo/trial, the full hotel-consultancy status flow, online payment, and website content management. (AM-11, CF-1, GP-23)

## AMPM's decision (2026-10-25)

The first release includes the demo and the subscription hotel-discovery workflow, wedding management after appointment, vendor selection, the client portal, department-based team access, RSVP, tracked requests, live chat and offline wedding-day operations. It excludes website editing, online milestone payments and GST invoicing. Only Eva's authorised personnel may approve operational exceptions; the authority must be configurable. Decided 26 October 2026: the whole first release goes live at once, in one launch.
