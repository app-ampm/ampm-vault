# WhatsApp and SMS providers

**What we need.** Who provides WhatsApp and SMS. Both carry registration lead times of two to six weeks, and OTP sign-in depends on SMS, so this gates the launch.

**Why it matters.** Chooses the adapters and starts the registration clock.

**Linked questions.** [[Q11 Guest and client communication channels]], [[Q13 Sign-in]]

## Answer

**Partly answered 26 October 2026**: staff sign in with a password plus an authenticator-app code and the identity service is Cognito (Q13 closed, A-13, T-6, U-9). The WhatsApp and SMS providers themselves (Q11, R15) are still to be chosen; the developer selects, AMPM owns the accounts.
