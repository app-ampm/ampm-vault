**Register.** [[data-model|Data model]] §0 · **Depends on.** [[Q07 Guest identity in the RSVP portal]]

## Assumed (23 October 2026)

The guest list is loaded first (client upload or AMPM entry). Each family group receives a unique tokenised RSVP link. Guests respond per person per function. A group may add accompanying members up to `family_groups.max_additional_guests` (default 0). There is no open registration. Dietary preference is collected for everyone; allergies and accessibility needs are optional and SENSITIVE. The client sees their own guest list except RESTRICTED and identity fields.

## If AMPM decides otherwise

One shared wedding link would require a guest self-identification step and a de-duplication queue; `rsvp_tokens.family_group_id` would become nullable.

## AMPM's decision (2026-10-25)

**Changed.** Links per side or one combined link (final structure open); guests identify themselves against the loaded list; no editing after submission; corrections through the RSVP team. Per-person counting, meal preference and allergies confirmed. Family representatives see summaries only and cannot edit the list.
