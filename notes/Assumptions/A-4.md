## Assumed (23 October 2026)

AMPM may earn a margin on vendor and hotel pricing. Every commercial record therefore stores client-facing amounts and internal cost in separate columns (`vendor_proposals`, `hotel_proposals`, `budget_lines`, `room_blocks`, `project_final_accounts`).
